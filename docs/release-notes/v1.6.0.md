## New

- **More devices.** Battery levels for the FNATIC BOLT, ROCCAT Syn Max Air, Wraith W1 SE, Razer Kraken
  V3 Pro and Razer Nari, Logitech G321 and G735, MCHOSE X9 and V9 Turbo+, BEITONG KP70, Flydigi Apex 6,
  GameSir G7 Pro 8K, HyperX Cloud Stinger 3, JBL Quantum 800, and keyboards on the SONiX 0C45:FEFC
  receiver (AULA F87 Pro V2C, AJAZZ AK820 MAX). The ROG Pelta and the IO Nova SE are also read on their
  USB cable. These come from what other BatteryHub projects found; most were checked on the real device
  there, none on ours yet. If yours reads wrong, please open a device report.
- **Steady percentages.** A level no longer jumps back and forth (60, 62, 61): on battery it only goes
  down, while charging it only goes up. A change that keeps coming is still shown. Turn it off in
  *Display settings*.
- **Fully charged notification.** BatteryHub tells you once when a charging device reaches 100%.
- **Quiet hours.** *No notifications from 10 pm to 8 am* keeps the night quiet.
- **Faster while charging.** A charging device is read every 15 seconds, so you see it fill up.
- **Icon colour per device.** Each device's menu has *Icon color*, with the brand colours to choose from.
- **Open the maker's app.** Double-click a device on the taskbar to open G HUB, Synapse, iCUE,
  SteelSeries GG, Armoury Crate or the Wraith web driver, or the device's menu entry. Without the app
  installed, its download page opens. A single click still opens the menu.
- **DPI bubble (experimental).** With *Show the new DPI when it changes* on, pressing your mouse's DPI
  button shows the new value in a small bubble above it. Logitech, Razer and Wraith W1 SE mice. Off by
  default.
- **Updates without asking.** With *Install updates without asking*, a new version found by the daily
  check is downloaded, checked against its signature and installed on its own. Per-user installs only.

## Changed

- Settings of a device not seen for 30 days (its place, name, alert level and colour) are forgotten, so
  a device plugged in once does not stay around. Turn this off in *Display settings*.

## Install

Download `BatteryHub-Setup-1.6.0.exe` below. If you already run a version from this repository, it
offers the update from its menu. Coming from the original BatteryHub? Install this version once by
hand; your settings and battery history are kept.
